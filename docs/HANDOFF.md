## Implemented gameplay and decisions

- App defaults to **Modern**, with saved Classic/Modern preference in Options. Rules changes discard progress and generate a fresh map of the same size. Restart repeats the original seed, size, faction count and rules.
- **Difficulty:** New Game offers Easy / Normal (default) / Hard. The saved preference applies to future matches; the match retains its own difficulty on restart and rules changes. Equal rules/resources at every level. Easy searches one movement ahead; Normal searches up to three coordinated actions; Hard searches up to five and samples rival replies. AI can hold position, forecasts threats through enemy routes, uses true wages, competes for mana across kingdoms and uses all six Modern spells. See `docs/ai-system.md` for implementation and limits.
- **Quick Play** separately selects **Map size**, **Kingdoms** and **Difficulty**. Defaults are Small/four kingdoms; counts include the player. Very Small supports **2–4** kingdoms, Small/Medium/Large **2–6**, preserving full seven-tile homes and at least two intervening hexes between starting borders. Changing size retains a compatible count or clamps it to the new maximum. Size still controls world bounds, terrain budgets and camera zoom; counts no longer follow size in the UI. Maps retain neutral land, irregular coast/lakes/corridors and connected passable terrain. Modern mountain rules are unchanged.
- Kingdom positions are sampled across the selected bounds, with faction assignment shuffled and **capital distance at least 5 hex steps**. Seven-hex starting rings therefore leave at least two intervening hexes between opposing borders; some starts are farther apart. Shape uses randomized connections between homes instead of a central hub, variable corridor width, offset neutral land bulges and erosion. Minimum expansion land is guaranteed. Same seed/size/rules remain reproducible, but seeds now produce different maps than the old generator.
- Each faction starts with a varied connected shape, one Scout and one mana source at its castle. Modern has six to nine provinces; Classic keeps seven. Modern starts with **10 G**, total starting province value **10**, and Scout/Soldier/Knight wages **3/9/27 G**. Classic retains 16 G, one income per clear tile, and wages 1/2/4. Recruitment costs **10/20/40 G** in both modes; upgrades pay the catalogue price difference.
- Equal-tier ready units merge into the next tier. Merged units and upgraded unmoved units can move immediately. Movement crosses any amount of friendly connected land/accessible neutral bridges, then captures one border province. Enemy capture requires strength **strictly greater** than defence. Knights defend themselves at 3 but lend at most 2 to adjacent friendly hexes, allowing a breach into formerly invulnerable pockets. Max unit tier/strength remains 3. Knight-on-Knight attacks kill both units without changing territory, capitals, structures or treasuries; only Knights allow equal-strength attacks, in both rulesets. Fortify absorbs the first hit before attrition. Playback and cancellation support the duel. See `docs/rebalancing-implementation-2026-10-15.md` for the 40 G implementation and 48-match comparison.
- Modern neutral provinces have value 1–3, earning that much gold when clear. Develop costs **2 × current value²**, raises value by one, capped at 6. Towers cost 15 G and provide defence 2; houses cost 20 G and add 4 income; structures have no upkeep.
- Trees spread; graves later become trees. Clearing trees by movement, recruitment or Summon pays **1 G in Modern**; graves pay nothing. Bankruptcy kills the affected kingdom's army, not every faction's army.
- **Bridges:** 15 G per adjacent empty-water tile; chains allowed. Wooden tiles stay neutral, with no income/value/recruitment/development. Intact bridge chains unite friendly land into one kingdom and pool treasuries/income/wages. Destruction separates kingdoms; the surviving capital keeps reserves. Hostile garrisons block movement but **do not break economic connectivity**—this is an intentional current decision. Recruitment/building are restricted to the selected kingdom.
- **Mana:** one faction-wide pool, starts at 1. Owned sources add 1 maximum mana, or 2 on capitals or wooded provinces (bonuses do not stack). Regeneration is 1 + floor(owned source count / 4) per faction turn, capped by storage. Clearing a wooded source clamps stored mana. Capturing a source increases capacity without instant mana; source loss clamps storage. Replacement capitals create no new sources.
- **Summon:** 2 mana, ready Scout on empty friendly kingdom land, clears trees/graves. **Fortify:** 3 mana, friendly structure or reachable neutral bridge; first valid hostile hit removes ward and spends the attacker’s move, preserving defenders/structure; second hit acts normally. Expires at caster's next turn. **Shatter:** 3 mana, reachable neutral structure; a ward absorbs the first cast, a later cast destroys the bridge and its garrison. **Trailblaze:** 2 mana, faction blessing; the next valid unit move consumes it and grants another move only when capturing neutral land (not a bridge). **Grove:** 1 mana, plants a tree on clear empty kingdom land or directly adjacent land, including enemy land. **Barrier:** 2 mana, clear empty kingdom land; blocks all units entering or crossing until caster's next turn. Each tile can be barriered once per game; history survives expiry and capture. No spell directly damages enemy units.
- Modern castle capture also annexes **only directly adjacent provinces of that same kingdom**, retaining buildings/resources and killing their garrisons. Distant land does not surrender.
- **Victory now occurs when no enemy capitals remain**, even with enemy singletons/stranded troops. Defeat still means losing all player land. The defeat popup offers Restart/Main Menu; departure waits for the choice.
