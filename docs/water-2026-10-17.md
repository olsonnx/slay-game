# Water styles - 2026-10-17

Options > Video > Stylized water enables the new shader. It defaults off, retaining the original calm water. The preference is saved and applies immediately in gameplay, the editor, and the menu. Missing or malformed saved values default to original water.

## Implementation

`resources/shaders/water_original.gdshader` preserves the previous sine-based shader. `resources/shaders/water.gdshader` adapts [MinhCreatorVN's Stylized Water for Godot 4.x](https://godotshaders.com/shader/stylized-water-for-godot-4-x/). Attribution and the upstream MIT license are in `resources/shaders/water.LICENSE.md`; no demo artwork is imported.

The adaptation adds seamless flowing noise, wave normals, broken crests, subtle refraction and depth-based contact foam. Depth reconstruction supports our orthographic cameras and Compatibility renderer. The surface remains flat and unshaded to match the board's presentation. Foam is narrow because tile walls extend only slightly below the water plane.

`src/rendering/water_surface.gd` creates independent materials with shared shader and procedural texture resources. Switching styles preserves the material's `backdrop_depth` state. Menu water and island departures keep the far-depth behavior that lets tiles fall visibly below the plane; depth foam/refraction are skipped in that mode.

## Tuning and previews

The stylized shader exposes color, deep_water, edge_color, speed, noise_scale, normal_strength, beer_law_factor, edge_scale and refraction_strength. time_override defaults to -1 (live time); nonnegative values freeze animation for captures.

Run `godot --path . --script tools/water_preview.gd` without --headless for gameplay, top-down, menu and animation captures in artifacts/water-after-*.png. The tool explicitly enables stylized water for those captures. If artifacts/water-before.gdshader exists it also captures the old shader. Original files are backed up in artifacts/water-before.zip.

## Validation

Godot 4.6.3 native OpenGL Compatibility rendering on NVIDIA RTX 2060: inspected both styles and the Video toggle at 960 x 620; no shader/script errors. The animation probe changed 55,810 of 60,000 sampled water pixels between times 12 and 15.

After adding the toggle: test_menus passed 52 checks, test_presentation passed 9,886, and test_ui_layout passed 183. Tests cover live switching, menu synchronization, persistence, legacy/malformed settings, shared textures and preservation of transition depth. The localization catalogue was regenerated. Earlier shader validation also passed test_menu_rendering, test_menu_background and test_level_editor.

The stylized option samples screen/depth textures and costs more rendering work than the original. No cross-device performance benchmark or non-Compatibility renderer validation has been performed.
