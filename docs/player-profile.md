# Kingdom profile and first launch

The first interactive launch opens **Create your kingdom**. A nonempty kingdom name is required; a motto is optional. One local profile is stored per installation. The bottom-right main-menu card and profile header show the signed-in Steam name, or **Local player** offline. The card matches the main menu panel's fill, outline and corners and shows a compact row with the account level and a slim progress bar. XP amounts remain in the full profile panel. The card updates when XP changes and opens the statistics view; that view's brush button opens customization. Cancel discards cosmetic edits without changing the saved profile.

The editor offers a country color, seven flag patterns, eight emblems including None, separate field/accent/emblem colors, emblem scale and seven placements. Customization is split into Country and Flag tabs. Colors use a wheel and six-digit hex field, without advanced sliders. Flags keep their original 3:2 proportions; emblems can be centered, placed directly left/right, or placed in any corner. The Country tab has a larger island preview using the game's tiles, units, lighting and materials. The narrower statistics card shows identity, account level and record without the country preview.

Quick Play's country picker offers the saved kingdom, random defaults, and a grid of named default countries. Choosing the saved kingdom applies its name and color to generated solo matches; choosing a default preserves that country's identity while still recording results in the profile. Authored maps, tutorials and multiplayer seat identities keep their own configurations.

The gameplay heading shows the active country's flag and name, with turn count and game mode underneath. A custom kingdom uses the flag saved at match start; defaults use their country colors. The heading follows hotseat and opponent turns, while World activity is indicated in the lower line.

After creation, a first-time prompt strongly recommends the tutorial. **Play tutorial** opens Armies and expansion; **Skip for now** enters the main menu. This decision persists, and all tutorial lessons remain accessible from the main menu. **Campaign** is visible but disabled until the campaign system exists.

The profile records completed solo and online matches: wins, losses, draws, elapsed active time, rounds, average match length, longest match and win streaks. Local hotseat, tutorials, editor playtests, replay viewing, abandoned games and restarted unfinished games do not alter the competitive record. Results are captured at completion, with per-match IDs preventing duplicate counting. Editing cosmetic details never resets statistics.

Eligible completed matches also award account XP. The base reward is 100 XP, multiplied by outcome (victory 1.5, defeat 0.75, draw 1), difficulty (easy 0.75, normal 1, hard 1.4), map size (Tiny 1, Small 1.25, Standard 1.6, Large 2, Huge 2.5), and opponent count (10% extra per opponent beyond the first, up to five opponents). Retired Very Small maps use 0.8. Mixed lobbies average actual opponent difficulty; human opponents use normal weight. Duration does not increase XP. The final award is rounded once. For example, a Standard victory against five Hard opponents awards 470 XP.

Accounts begin at level 1. Level 2 requires 250 XP; each subsequent level costs 100 XP more than the previous one. The profile shows current level, total XP and progress to the next level. Results animate the saved award through level boundaries and announce level-ups. Reopening results cannot award XP again. XP saves atomically with the match record and resets when the profile is deleted. Older profiles retain their records and begin at level 1 with zero XP; past results are not awarded retroactively.

The profile is stored as `user://player_profile.cfg`. Writes use a temporary file and preserve a `.bak` copy; loading can recover from that backup. **Delete profile** in the statistics view requires confirmation, removes the profile and its recovery files, clears its record and tutorial progress, and returns to fresh profile creation. This is the player's local identity and progress save; it does not yet resume an unfinished match or synchronize through the cloud.

App fixtures explicitly set `onboarding_enabled = false` when testing existing menu/game behavior. `tests/test_account_progress.gd` covers level boundaries, reward scaling, mixed lobbies, migration, duplicate awards, persistence and failed-save rollback. `tests/test_player_profile.gd` exercises onboarding, Steam names, statistics/edit navigation, color previews, custom/default Quick Play choices, result recording, XP presentation, recovery, confirmed deletion and starting fresh. `tools/player_profile_preview.gd` captures the creator, recommendation, main menu, statistics, editor, color wheel, country grid, deletion confirmation and a level-up results screen using an isolated profile file.
