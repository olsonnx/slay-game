# Hold to speed up playback

Hold **Ctrl** during a player or opponent move for **6× animation speed**. While opponents play, the loading End turn button also accepts a hold. Its faction name gains `»` during accelerated playback. Releasing both controls restores normal speed immediately. Moving the pointer off the held button stops that hold. Pause, focus loss, closing and completion restore normal speed. A consumed loading-button press remains consumed through completion/pause, so its later release cannot start another round; the next fresh press behaves normally.

`src/ui/playback_speed.gd` owns the hold state, global Ctrl/release events, loading-button signals and focus/pause cleanup. It runs while paused to clear holds, without advancing presentation. The button stays interactive only during eligible opponent processing; recruitment, building and other game commands remain locked. `game.turn_button_activated` guards its normal end-turn callback. Speed-only releases do not play extra click sounds.

`BoardPlayback.scaled_tween` registers movement, approach/jab/retreat, preparation, action ripples, castle surrender and the 0.18 s gap between opponent factions. `set_speed_scale` adjusts registered live tweens when the rate changes; new tweens inherit that rate. Invalid tween references are pruned on registration/rate changes, so the list does not accumulate across rounds. Cancellation still kills its existing motion/preparation/annex tweens, preserves revision guards and restores authoritative state. Phase-gap tweens remain owned by the game so shutdown can unwind the existing coroutine safely.

No global `Engine.time_scale` change is used. Camera navigation/following, menu transitions, spinner cadence, sound pitch, economic calculations, RNG and AI search retain their original timing/policy. AI planning CPU work is not accelerated; a loading button can still show thinking time even while held. At 6×, 30 ordinary 0.22 s travel steps take about 1.1 seconds rather than 6.6 seconds, excluding other phases and frame scheduling. Rate checks are constant work each frame; only rate changes rescale the current tweens.

Verification: all 18 suites from `test_animation` onward passed, including 40 animation, 42 input, 17 turn-feedback, 5,080 presentation, 160 events/spells, 100 UI layout and the then-26 speed checks. The final speed fixture passed **30 checks**, including mid-move acceleration/release, pre-held Ctrl, fast cancellation/RNG restoration, overlapping holds, pause/focus loss, dragging away, completion-release guards, scaled preparation, fast combat/castle-surrender stage ordering and a real accelerated opponent round started through the turn-button callback. Existing simulation-only AI/general suites were not repeated for this presentation change. Godot editor scan passed.

Native previews inspected: `artifacts/ui_help.png` at 960 × 620, and `artifacts/preview_fast_turn_water_modern_turn_busy.png`. Reproduce the latter with `tools/preview.gd -- --modern --water --turn-busy --fast-turn`. Run `tests/test_playback_speed.gd` for the dedicated regression fixture.
