SLAY & REIGN - CLOSED PLAYTEST

Thanks for trying the game. This build tests the new cities, recruitment and
roads rules. The exact version and playtest label are in BUILD.txt.

STARTING THE GAME
1. Extract the entire ZIP into a normal folder before launching.
2. Open "Slay and Reign.exe". Keep the other files beside it.
3. Use a Windows x64 PC with a graphics driver supporting Direct3D 12.
   Hardware compatibility is still being tested; no minimum spec is established.
   You do not need Godot installed. Steam is optional for solo/local play.
If Windows or your security software blocks the build, tell us the exact message.

YOUR FIRST SESSION (ABOUT 30-45 MINUTES)
Start with Tutorial: Armies and expansion, Kingdoms and money, then Cities and
supply lines. Try Quick Play on Tiny with 2 kingdoms and Easy difficulty.
Play at your own pace; you do not have to finish. Tell us where you needed help
before looking for an explanation. Try other settings afterward if you want.

WHAT WE WANT TO LEARN
- Can you tell where you can move and recruit, and why an action is unavailable?
- Do income, army wages and infrastructure upkeep make sense?
- When do cities and roads feel useful? Which decision was most interesting?
- Did you want another match? What made you stop?

QUICK CONTROLS (CHANGE THEM IN OPTIONS > CONTROLS)
Left click: select a province/unit, then a highlighted destination.
Right click: cancel. Right mouse drag: rotate/tilt camera.
Mouse wheel: zoom. Middle mouse drag or WASD: pan.
Q / E / R: Recruit / Build / Spells; also choose cards in an open menu.
Space: end turn. Tab: overhead/3D view. F: value heatmap.
Escape: cancel or pause. Hold Ctrl: faster movement playback.
The ? button shows help. F3 toggles the FPS display.
The music-note button at the top right opens the track name, pause and next
controls. Music volume is in Options > Audio.
Options > Interface includes UI scale, reduced motion and hover-help timing.
Options > Game includes camera sensitivity and movement playback speed.

CURRENT LIMITATIONS
- There is no mid-match save/load. Leaving or restarting loses that match.
- Restart repeats the same starting map.
- Replays can only be saved after a completed match, from the results screen.
- Steam online is experimental and currently uses development App ID 480
  (Spacewar). Start with solo play. A separate online test needs the same build
  and different signed-in Steam accounts; reconnect is not supported.
- Performance on other PCs and large maps still needs testing.
